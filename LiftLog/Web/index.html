Calorie target

Your target comes from your TDEE, the calories you burn in a day. The range runs from extreme loss (about 2 lb a week) up to mild loss (about ½ lb a week).

Your data

One CSV file holds everything: workouts, cardio, weigh-ins and food. Export one now and then as a backup; deleting the app deletes the log. Settings and My foods aren't in the file.

Privacy

Lift Log has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your log stays on this phone. The only thing that ever leaves it is a CSV you choose to export. Food estimates run on the iPhone itself and never go online.
